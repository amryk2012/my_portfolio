About this project

A VR-based medical training system combining Unity, AI, and interactive haptic gloves, so medical students can practice clinical procedures on virtual patients in a safe, risk-free environment. It was selected among the top 33 startups in ITIDA's Kreativa Incubation Program (3rd cohort), in collaboration with the Faculty of Engineering and the Faculty of Medicine at Suez Canal University.

Key features

  • VR training environment built in Unity
  • Interactive haptic gloves
  • AI-driven virtual patients
  • Safe, risk-free practice of clinical procedures
  • Top 33 startups, ITIDA Kreativa Incubation Program (3rd cohort)
