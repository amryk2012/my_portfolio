About this project

A full-featured multiplayer first-person shooter built from scratch in Unity. Players log in through a backend database that tracks their statistics, then play across multiple arenas against other players, AI-controlled bots, or waves of zombies. Multiplayer is handled with Photon Networking.

Key features

  • Multiplayer gameplay with Photon Networking
  • Multiple weapon types: sniper rifles, SMGs, shotguns, and rifles
  • Customizable weapon system: fire rate, spread, and projectile count
  • Player login with database-based statistics
  • AI-controlled bots
  • Waves mode
  • Zombie survival mode
  • Multiple arenas

My contribution

Built the project from scratch: the modular weapon system, player login and database integration, bot AI, the game modes, and the Photon multiplayer layer.
